How I decide

Five questions before any design

  1. Start with the business goalWhat must this brand make people do?
  2. Know the buyerWho signs off, and what are they afraid of?
  3. Own one clear positionOne sentence. One promise. Said the same way everywhere.
  4. Build a system, not a one-offColour, type, tone and templates the whole team can use.
  5. Let data decideTest, measure, keep what converts and cut the rest.
